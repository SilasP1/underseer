Thistle / Distributed seismic sensing

Detect activity beyond the camera.

Thistle listens for ground activity and cues the cameras, sensors, and operators already on site.

PrototypeHardware + edge sensingEarly field testing
01 / System role

A cue layer for uncovered ground.

Thistle watches the ground for activity cameras cannot see. It is designed to send time and place to the rest of the security stack.

01

Observes

Detects ground-coupled activity across a distributed field.

02

Reports

Packages each detection as a small, time-stamped event.

03

Supports

Cues cameras, radar, DAS, VMS, or an operator.

02 / How it fits

Sense. Cue. Verify.

Thistle does not replace high-detail sensors. It tells them where to look.

Sense

03 / Intended environments

Where coverage runs out.

01

Mobile & temporary security

Temporary sites without permanent monitoring.

02

Infrastructure corridors

Long assets that are difficult to watch continuously.

03

Field research & evaluation

Representative sites used to measure what the system can and cannot do.

04 / Development status

Prototype.

Thistle is in bench and early field testing. Current work is focused on signal quality, low-power architecture, and performance across different sites.

Range depends on soil, installation, activity, and noise. Underseer has not published a production range.
